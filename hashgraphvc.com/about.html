About Beco

Crafting Structures with Conviction

Beco Contracting (Building Artisans Contracting) was established in 2020 as a premier engineering and construction contracting firm in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. We deliver integrated solutions characterized by premium engineering quality, technical innovation, and absolute commitment to safety and environmental standards. We take pride in building the structures that support the growth of Saudi communities.

Our Vision

"To be the leading contracting company in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, by providing integrated engineering solutions characterized by quality, innovation, and commitment to the highest standards of safety and sustainability. We seek to contribute to the realization of Saudi Vision 2030 through developing modern urban projects that meet the needs of our clients with the highest levels of professionalism and efficiency. We aspire to build a better future by executing residential, commercial, and industrial projects and facilities of international standards, to be the first choice in the construction and contracting sector."
